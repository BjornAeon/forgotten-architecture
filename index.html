The Ancient Reality of the Modern World

Firstly, let us explore the possibility that we have been lied to about almost everything and virtually since the very beginning of all.

Put together in a way for essentially everyone. To understand the origins of not only the human race but also the cosmos itself.

About the Author

Bjorn Aeolus grew up in the woods of Northeast Texas, between the towns of Quitman and Hawkins. He has spent most of his life studying and cross-referencing the world’s conflicting accounts of where humanity and the cosmos came from, and this book is his attempt to put those findings together for everyone.
